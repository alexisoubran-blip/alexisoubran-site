LATAM performance marketing

Plan regional acquisition with country-level economics, localized messages and a consistent framework for commercial reporting.

How should brands plan performance marketing in Latin America?

A regional program needs shared commercial definitions and decisions that respect local markets. Mexico, Colombia, Chile, Argentina and Peru can differ in demand, pricing, payment methods, retail channels and sales operations.

Alexis Soubran’s work connects regional planning with local performance, creative and measurement through Minimalist Agency. The scope depends on the markets and execution resources required.

Set one regional reporting standard

Define qualified outcomes, attribution windows and conversion stages consistently. Report regional spend and results together, then retain country-level detail to understand the variance.

Document exchange rates and reporting currency. Compare customer acquisition against contribution margin and actual collections in each market before reallocating capital.

Localize the offer and conversion path

  • Confirm local availability, pricing, delivery and service coverage.
  • Adapt creative to buyer objections, language and product familiarity.
  • Check landing pages, mobile usability, payment options and CRM response times.
  • Use local sales feedback to evaluate whether leads have purchase intent.

Allocate budget in stages

Start in markets where the offer, fulfillment and measurement are ready. Assign a defined pilot budget and evaluation window. Test budget changes against incremental qualified outcomes, and include sales-cycle delays when interpreting results.

A regional average may conceal a market with slow payback or weak qualification. Keep those differences visible in the investment review.

Frequently asked questions

Should LATAM campaigns use the same message in every country?

Use a common positioning where it fits, then adapt the offer, language, proof and conversion path to local buyer needs and product availability.

How should regional performance be compared?

Use consistent outcome definitions, reporting currency and attribution windows. Keep country-level detail for CAC, contribution margin, qualification and payback so a regional total does not hide local problems.
