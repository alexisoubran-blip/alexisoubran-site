FAQ

Questions the framework should answer.

Direct definitions help teams align before briefs, creator negotiations and media plans begin.

What is Perfluencer Marketing?

Perfluencer Marketing is a creator-commerce framework that combines influencer storytelling with performance distribution, measurement and commercial feedback.

How is it different from traditional influencer marketing?

Traditional influencer programs often stop at reach and engagement. Perfluencer Marketing connects creator content to paid amplification, search demand, conversion quality, ROAS and revenue learning.

Which metrics does it use?

The metric set depends on the business model, but can include WER, search lift, qualified traffic, CPA, ROAS, conversion value, creator-level efficiency and new-customer outcomes.

Is it only for ecommerce brands?

No. The framework can support ecommerce, retail, lead generation and launch programs as long as there is a measurable commercial signal and a way to feed results back into creator and media decisions.
