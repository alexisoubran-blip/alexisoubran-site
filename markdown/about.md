## Who is Alexis Soubran?

Alexis Soubran is a Mexico City-based marketing executive, CEO and partner at Minimalist Agency, author, educator and speaker. His work focuses on performance marketing, market entry in Mexico and Latin America, creator campaigns and marketing measurement.

He teaches at Escuela de Mercadotecnia (EDEM) and has participated in the Merca2.0 National Digital Marketing Congress. His book, [Oops, I Bought It: What's Behind Influencer Marketing](https://alexisoubran.com/book/), describes his approach to connecting creator storytelling and performance measurement.

## Codifying what works so it can compound.

I created [Perfluencer Marketing](https://alexisoubran.com/perfluencer-marketing/), a framework that fuses influencer marketing with performance accountability. The methodology connects creator strategy, paid amplification, audience segmentation, EMV, lift studies, ROAS, and ethical influence guidelines.

The framework is documented in my book [Oops, I Bought It: What's Behind Influencer Marketing](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0F7GQM972).
