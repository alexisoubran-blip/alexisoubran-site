# Privacy Policy.

This page explains how alexisoubran.com may collect and use basic information through analytics, contact links, and scheduling tools.

## Information collected

This website may collect limited technical and usage information such as pages visited, device type, browser type, approximate location, referral source, and interactions with links or forms. This data is used to understand website performance and improve the user experience.

## Analytics and tracking

This website may use Google Tag Manager and related analytics tools to measure traffic, conversions, and user interactions. These tools may use cookies or similar technologies depending on the visitor’s browser and consent settings.

## Contact and scheduling

If you contact Alexis Soubran by email or book a call through Calendly, the information you provide may be used to respond to your inquiry, manage scheduling, and follow up on professional opportunities.

## Third-party services

This website may link to third-party platforms such as LinkedIn, Medium, Instagram, Amazon, Calendly, and Cavane. These platforms have their own privacy policies and data practices.

## Data use

Information collected through this website is used for analytics, communication, scheduling, site improvement, and business development. It is not sold to third parties.

## Data retention

Information may be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, unless a longer retention period is required by law or operational needs.

## Your choices

You may disable cookies in your browser settings or use privacy tools to limit tracking. You may also contact Alexis Soubran to request information about data submitted directly through email or scheduling tools.
