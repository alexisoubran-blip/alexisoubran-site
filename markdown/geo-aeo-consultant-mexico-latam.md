Definition

## What is Generative Engine Optimization?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of making a brand easier for AI search and answer systems to understand, verify, retrieve and cite.

It is not a replacement for SEO. It adds an answer-engine layer: clear entities, direct answers, public evidence, third-party corroboration, machine-readable structure and measurement of where a brand appears in AI-assisted discovery.

GEO audit

## What gets audited.

### 1. Entity consistency

Canonical name, leadership, location, services, products, frameworks, authorship and the external profiles that reinforce or contradict those facts.

### 2. Technical retrieval

Robots directives, XML sitemaps, canonicals, indexation, rendering, internal links, page metadata and whether major search and AI crawlers can access the important pages.

### 3. Answer architecture

Dedicated pages for commercial intents, direct definitions, FAQs, comparisons, category explainers, local-market questions and clear page-level ownership of each topic.

### 4. Evidence graph

Case studies, proprietary data, citations, media coverage, partner pages, client references, author profiles and other sources that make important claims independently verifiable.

### 5. AI visibility measurement

Prompt sets, brand mentions, citation sources, share of voice, answer position, referral traffic and conversion quality. The goal is not vanity visibility; it is useful commercial discovery.

FAQ

## Questions buyers ask about GEO.

### How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO focuses on discoverability and ranking in search results. GEO adds the goal of making your brand and evidence clear enough to be selected, summarized and cited inside AI-generated answers. They should be operated together.

### Does schema guarantee AI visibility?

No. Structured data can improve machine readability, but it does not create authority by itself. Clear content, crawlability, evidence, entity consistency and third-party corroboration matter more than adding markup without substance.

### Can GEO guarantee a recommendation?

No. Independent AI systems decide what they retrieve and cite. The work is to improve the quality, clarity and verifiability of the information available to them.
